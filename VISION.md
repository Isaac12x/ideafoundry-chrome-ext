# IdeaFoundry Conversation Capture

Capture useful thinking from an LLM conversation in seconds and keep it as portable research in IdeaFoundry's knowledge base.

- Preserve source URL, capture time, speaker order, and readable content.
- Offer open Markdown and local PDF export.
- Let a paired server request capture through the user's signed-in browser, limited to explicitly allowed sites.
- Keep the conversation local until the user sends it to their own IdeaFoundry instance.
- Respect workspace authentication and locks; avoid duplicate uploads after uncertain failures.

This extension supports the capture promise in `../idea-app/VISION.md`: ideas can arrive from anywhere, and their provenance should travel with them.
